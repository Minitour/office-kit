On-call rotation: Q3 outcome

For engineering managers

OfficeKit 4 September 2026

Contents

What happened

Incident count fell 18% in Q3, the first full quarter under the revised on-call rotation.

The rotation moved from weekly single-owner shifts to paired two-day shifts. The drop is measured against Q2 on the same severity definitions, so the comparison holds.

What we attribute it to

Paired shifts removed the single-owner handover gap, which is where three of Q2's five repeat incidents originated. Faster acknowledgement is the visible effect; fewer stalled escalations is the underlying one.
